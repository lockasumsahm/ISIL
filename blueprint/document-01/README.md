# ISIL — Executive Overview

## What Is ISIL

ISIL (Internet Safety Intelligence Layer) is not a
moderation API. It is not a fraud detector. It is not
a cybersecurity product. It is not an AI model.

ISIL is a global trust infrastructure platform that
helps digital systems make safer, more accurate, more
explainable, and more trustworthy decisions.

Think:
- Cloudflare protects infrastructure
- Stripe protects payments
- ISIL protects trust itself

## Why ISIL Exists

Every digital platform faces the same unsolved problem.

Existing solutions fail because:
- Single models make final decisions
- No context understanding
- No jurisdiction intelligence
- No explanation for decisions
- High false positive rates
- Cannot handle multilingual content
- No audit trail

ISIL solves all of these simultaneously.

## Mission

MAXIMUM CORRECTNESS UNDER UNCERTAINTY

Trust is the product.
Accuracy is the product.
Explainability is the product.

## Vision

To become the global standard trust infrastructure
layer for digital platforms — the system that makes
every digital interaction safer, more accurate, and
more explainable.

## Core Objectives

1. Maximize decision correctness
2. Minimize false positives
3. Minimize false negatives
4. Provide full explainability for every decision
5. Support every jurisdiction globally
6. Protect privacy by design
7. Enable human oversight at every level
8. Scale to billions of decisions per day

## Non-Goals

ISIL does not:
- Replace human judgment on high-stakes decisions
- Make autonomous legal determinations
- Store raw user content
- Provide a surveillance infrastructure
- Make irreversible decisions without exceptional evidence

## Success Metrics

- Decision correctness rate
- False positive rate
- False negative rate
- Calibration error
- Explanation quality score
- Latency per decision
- Appeal success rate
- Human review rate
- Provider agreement rate
- System availability

## Intended Users

- Platform developers integrating safety APIs
- Trust and safety engineering teams
- Government and regulatory bodies
- Enterprise security teams
- Independent researchers

## Product Philosophy

Every decision must be:
- Explainable
- Challengeable
- Reversible
- Auditable
- Measurable

No black boxes. No unexplained enforcement.
No single model authority.
